# FireShark Community Guidelines

This is the source text rendered at `/community-guidelines`. The page is defined in
`frontend/src/pages/Static.tsx` (`CommunityGuidelines`) — update it there and rebuild the
frontend. Admin-editable site copy (site name, description, support URL) lives in
**Admin → Settings**.

---

## What belongs here

- Practical technical questions on cybersecurity, ethical hacking, penetration testing,
  networking, cloud security, SOC & blue team work, forensics and more.
- Certification preparation and career guidance.
- Hands-on lab and project troubleshooting.
- Clear answers backed by experience, references or working commands.

## How to ask a good question

- Use a descriptive title that summarises the actual problem.
- Include what you are trying to accomplish, what you already tried, and the exact error or
  output.
- Share your environment: operating system, tool and version.
- Format commands and output in code blocks.
- For networking questions, include topology and addressing details (sanitised).

## What is not allowed

- Publishing passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys or other credentials.
- Personal data: phone numbers, addresses, government IDs, confidential company information.
- Requests for unauthorised access, credential theft, malware distribution or any illegal
  activity.
- Doxxing, harassment, phishing promotion or malicious links.
- Spam, advertising and excessive self-promotion.
- Account recovery, payment disputes and order problems — these go to
  [FireShark Support](https://fireshark.in/), not the community.

Legitimate offensive-security education (labs, CTFs, authorised engagements) is welcome —
scope your discussions to environments you are authorised to test.

## Voting, reputation and badges

- Upvote answers that genuinely help; downvote only when something is incorrect or unsafe,
  ideally with a comment explaining why.
- Vote based on usefulness, not agreement.
- Reputation reflects community usefulness — ask, answer and get accepted to earn it.
- Badges recognise milestones like first questions, accepted answers and sustained
  contribution.
- The monthly leaderboard resets on the 1st; history is preserved.

## Moderation & reporting

- Use the Report link on any question, answer or comment that breaks the rules.
- Moderators may hide, close or remove content and take action on accounts.
- Closed questions remain visible so knowledge is not lost.
- Significant moderation actions are logged.

## Privacy

- Share only what you are comfortable making public.
- Remove personal information from screenshots before uploading.
- Profiles are public; location and website are optional.
